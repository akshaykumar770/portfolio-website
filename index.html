About

Editing as craft

Every frame tells a story. Every cut carries weight. The difference between ordinary footage and exceptional content lives in the details — timing, pacing, color, sound — and how those elements work together to move an audience.

With six years editing across industries, I've developed a philosophy: great editing serves the message, never distracts from it. Whether it's a 15-second Reel or a 10-minute brand film, every project gets the same level of intentionality and precision.

I work best with brands that understand content isn't an afterthought — it's central to how you communicate, connect, and convert.
